Open Data Project

The stories hiding in public data.

Public institutions collect this data with public money, so it deserves to be seen. Every figure can be verified by anyone, through the same official source. These are the stories that portal spreadsheets never tell.

The projects

Each project charts one public dataset, one question at a time. Live projects open straight from their tile; the rest are still in research.

  • Live

    Dutch Fleet Electrification Explorer

    How electric the Dutch vehicle fleet really is, charted question by question from the RDW open register of vehicles.

  • Live

    Dutch Courtroom Explorer

    What the Dutch courts rule on, charted question by question from the open data of the Dutch judiciary.

  • In research

    Green Grid Atlas

    The Dutch and European electricity grid on open grid data: where power flows, and where the grid strains.

  • Planned

    Housing and Energy Transition Explorer

    The Dutch housing stock and its energy transition, built on open government data.

  • More to come

    New projects are always in research.

Across the projects, AI explains what the charts show, always labelled experimental, with every figure computed from the official source.

Home Energy Dashboard

A self-built home energy dashboard, not an open data project, but it follows the same discipline as everything in this family: the server computes from the official data, and the model only narrates what the numbers mean.
